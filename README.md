Do you have modded medicines, or mods that require tiers of medicines you dont have in your game? Customize the required medicine tier for surgeries. Reassign any surgery (vanilla or modded) to any medicine category you choose.

To use, open the mod options from in game. You'll see a list of medicine tiers and surgeries, and you can change them per-category or per-surgery.
